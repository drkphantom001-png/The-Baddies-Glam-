## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an ultra-luxe, high-fashion mobile beauty experience tailored for clients seeking premier makeup artistry, bespoke glam sessions, and celebratory transformations. The aesthetic fuses Parisian editorial elegance with playful, radiant femininity.

The visual style is characterized by luminous soft gradients, arched portrait framing reminiscent of vanity mirrors, and glowing pill components. The interface evokes feelings of pampering, self-expression, exclusivity, and effortless booking. Crisp plum typography grounds the airy, blush-tinted canvas, while delicate warm gold sparkle accents (✦) provide celebratory micro-interactions. The experience strictly maintains a luminous, high-key light mode to preserve the signature radiant studio atmosphere.

## Colors

The palette establishes an unapologetically feminine, editorial tone with rigorous legibility:

- **Primary (`#d13c85`)**: Hot rose used for primary conversion buttons, selected states, and impactful accent badges.
- **Secondary (`#ee6aa7`)**: Vivid pink utilized for supporting action gradients, secondary interactive states, and glowing pill highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#e3a93f`)**: Warm polished gold reserved exclusively for sparkle highlights (✦), star ratings, VIP designations, and luxury foil accents.
- **Neutral (`#4a2437`)**: Deep plum, an ultra-rich alternative to pure black that supplies razor-sharp editorial contrast for body copy, titles, and structural iconography.

### Canvas & Surface Layers
- **App Canvas Gradient**: Linear vertical flow from `#fff5fa` at the crest to `#ffe1ee` at the base.
- **Surface Panels & Cards**: Translucent blush `#ffc4dc` at 30%–60% opacity with backdrop blur, or solid `#fff9fc` for elevated appointment cards.
- **Borders & Dividers**: Delicate rose mist (`rgba(209, 60, 133, 0.12)`) preserving visual order without introducing heavy lines.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy juxtaposes classical editorial poise with effortless legibility:

- **Headlines (`EB Garamond`)**: Conveys timeless haute-couture beauty editorial traditions. Used in generous line heights for service menus, page introductions, and modal headings.
- **Body & Controls (`Outfit`)**: Rendered in light weights (`300`) for paragraphs and descriptions to keep layouts breezy and modern, stepping up to medium (`500`) for uppercase labels, prices, and navigation targets.
- **Expressive Flourish Rules**: Accent callouts, such as "Glam", are styled using cursive italics with ample padding to prevent collision with bounding boxes.
- **Editorial Subtitles**: Categorical labels and eyebrow text employ uppercase `label-md` with tracking (`letter-spacing: 0.08em`) set in deep plum (`#4a2437`) or vibrant hot rose (`#d13c85`).

## Layout & Spacing

Designed fundamentally for handheld mobile screens, expanding into single-column centered tablet sheets (max-width `540px` for mobile frame simulation on larger displays):

- **Grid Architecture**: 4-column fluid layout on mobile screens with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` outer canvas padding.
- **Vertical Spacing Rhythm**: Section separations leverage generous vertical margins (`2.25rem` to `3rem`) allowing beauty imagery and glam package cards breathing room.
- **Safe Area Insets**: Explicit bottom clearance of `5.5rem` on all scrollable screens to prevent fixed floating tab bars from obscuring action triggers.
- **Touch Targets**: Minimum hit bounds are calibrated to `48px` height on all buttons, tabs, and booking calendar date chips.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through translucent layered rosiness and radiant illumination rather than harsh gray shadows:

- **Luminous Rose Shadow**: Primary action elements cast a vibrant pink aura: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(209, 60, 133, 0.28)`.
- **Frosted Translucent Layering**: Floating modules, bottom sheets, and the mobile bottom navigation bar feature `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` over semi-transparent white/blush backing (`rgba(255, 245, 250, 0.85)`).
- **Surface Elevation (Cards & Sheets)**: Lifted cards use a gentle, diffuse blush shade: `0 4px 20px rgba(74, 36, 55, 0.05)`.
- **Glint & Highlight Borders**: Raised surfaces receive a top-lit interior border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)` paired with an outer border `1px solid rgba(209, 60, 133, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The form language is soft, curvilinear, and organic:

- **Pill Philosophy**: Buttons, search inputs, active filter chips, time slots, and category pills are fully rounded pill shapes (`border-radius: 9999px`).
- **Arch-Framed Media**: Visual portraits, lookbook previews, and service highlights feature signature vanity arch framing (`border-radius: 9999px 9999px 1rem 1rem` or `border-radius: 120px 120px 1.5rem 1.5rem`).
- **Cards & Sheets**: Bottom navigation trays and service feature cards utilize `rounded-2xl` (`1.5rem` to `2rem`) top corners to reinforce softness.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Pill)**: Linear gradient background (`135deg, #ee6aa7 0%, #d13c85 100%`), pure white bolded text, full pill curvature, heightened with luminous rose ambient shadow. Active state creates an inward micro-scale of `0.98`.
- **Secondary (Ghost Blush)**: Border `1.5px solid #d13c85`, background `rgba(255, 196, 220, 0.25)`, text color `#d13c85`.
- **Gold Accent Buttons**: Reserved for VIP glam booking or bridal packages; features a subtle warm gold micro-border (`#e3a93f`) with star glyph `✦`.

### Bottom Navigation Bar (Client App PWA)
- **Structure**: Fixed to bottom viewport, frosted glass pill or full-width dock with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` and background `rgba(255, 245, 250, 0.9)`.
- **Items**: 5 designated client tabs: **Home**, **Services**, **Gallery**, **Book**, **Contact**.
- **Active State**: Active icon and label tinted with hot rose `#d13c85`, accompanied by a miniature gold sparkle dot `✦` indicator directly beneath the active label. Inactive items use deep plum at 45% opacity (`rgba(74, 36, 55, 0.45)`).

### Service Cards & Arch Media Frames
- **Card Container**: Soft blush surface (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`), `rounded-xl`, subtle hairline border in rose mist.
- **Service Image**: Anchored within a classic vanity arch cutout.
- **Meta Content**: Headline in Garamond, duration and price in bold plum `Outfit`, accompanied by a pill button labeled "Select Time".

### Chips & Booking Slots
- **Category Filter Chips**: Pill-shaped, light blush fill (`#fff0f7`), deep plum label. On selection: filled with `#d13c85`, white label, accompanied by soft pink glow.
- **Time Slot Pickers**: Rounded pill blocks with duration tags; unavailable slots grayed out with subtle strikeout plum tints.

### Form Inputs
- **Text & Date Fields**: Generous pill fields (`padding: 0.875rem 1.25rem`), soft border in `rgba(209, 60, 133, 0.2)`, background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.7)`. Focus state produces a `2px` focus ring in `#ee6aa7` and white background fill.
- **Placeholders**: Set in `Outfit` light italic plum tint (`rgba(74, 36, 55, 0.4)`).

### Sparkle Badges & Accents
- Decorative elements utilize `#e3a93f` for micro sparkles (✦) positioned alongside headings, customer ratings (e.g., `✦ 5.0 Glam Rating`), and exclusive packages.
